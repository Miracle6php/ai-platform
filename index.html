WHAT EACH STUDIO DOES

Three studios, each built for a different moment.

Face Studio NON-REALTIME

Record first, transform after. Best for pre-recorded video where you want full control over the result.

  • Swap identity to any character
  • Change or remove background
  • Restyle outfit and appearance

Voice Studio BOTH MODES

Convert your voice into another, either live as you speak or applied afterward to existing audio.

  • Real-time voice conversion
  • Non-realtime voice transformation
  • Consistent tone across long recordings

Live Studio REALTIME ONLY

Face and voice transformation together, live, with no post-processing step in between.

  • Combined face + voice, in sync
  • Stream to OBS or straight from browser
  • No delay between you and your audience

Streaming as a character
